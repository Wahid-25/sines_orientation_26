Welcome to Your New Academic Home

You have taken the first step into SINES — a place where biology meets technology, ideas turn into innovation, and curiosity drives discovery.

SINES

Where Different Fields Come Together

The School of Interdisciplinary Engineering & Sciences (SINES) at NUST brings together science, engineering, and technology to address real-world challenges through innovation and collaboration.

Established in 2007, SINES has evolved into a hub for interdisciplinary education, advanced research, and technological development.

Your Journey

The next four years start here

You are now part of the SINES community. The next four years will bring new discoveries, challenges, friendships, and unforgettable experiences.

At SINES, learning extends beyond traditional boundaries. Students are encouraged to explore different disciplines, collaborate across fields, and develop innovative solutions.
