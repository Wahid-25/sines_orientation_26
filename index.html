Navigation log

From Research Centre to Innovation Hub

SINES has evolved with changing technologies and global challenges into a community of students, researchers, and educators working toward innovation and meaningful impact.

2007

The Beginning

RCMS was established. A vision was created to bring modelling, simulation, and interdisciplinary research together under one platform.

2008

First Expansion

Computational Sciences and Engineering takes shape, opening opportunities for advanced computational research.

2010

New Frontiers

Bioinformatics enters the ecosystem, connecting computational approaches with biological sciences.

2012

Powering Innovation

High Performance Computing Facility established to strengthen research, simulation, and data-driven exploration.

2013

Building Expertise

Systems Engineering joins the journey, introducing a new dimension of interdisciplinary engineering.

2020

Accelerating Discovery

HPC Facility upgraded to support large-scale research and innovation.

2022

A New Identity

RCMS becomes SINES, marking a new era of interdisciplinary education, research, and collaboration.

2023

The Bioinformatics Era

BS and PhD Bioinformatics introduced.

2025

The Future of Discovery

SINES continues to bridge biology, computing, and engineering by preparing students to explore emerging technologies and solve future challenges.
